📐 Multiple Linear Regression (MLR) Transparency & Mathematical Blueprint

Full Explainability

The Mathematical Model

$$\hat{Y} = \beta_0 + \sum_{i=1}^{11} \beta_i \cdot \text{scale}(X_i) + \sum_{k=1}^{K} \gamma_k \cdot \text{OneHot}(\text{City}_k)$$

The model fits an Ordinary Least Squares hyperplane minimizing the sum of squared residuals: $\min_{\beta} \sum (y_i - \hat{y}_i)^2$.

  • Numerical Preprocessing: Median imputation followed by standard z-score normalization ($z = \frac{x - \mu}{\sigma}$).
  • Categorical Preprocessing: Most-frequent imputation and drop-first dummy encoding to prevent multicollinearity (dummy variable trap).
  • Training/Test Split: 80% train (3,640 records) vs 20% holdout test (911 records), evaluated with strictly zero leakage.

Holdout Test Evaluation Metrics (vs Naive Baseline)

R² (Coefficient of Determination) 0.676 67.6% of price variance explained
MAE (Mean Absolute Error) $131,870 vs $243,548 baseline (-45.8% error)
RMSE (Root Mean Sq Error) $219,399 vs $385,712 baseline
Baseline Comparison: A naive model predicting the historical mean achieves $R^2 \approx 0.00$ and MAE of $243,548. Our MLR model provides a 45.8% reduction in average error, verifying strong statistical significance across living area, city location, waterfront status, and bathrooms.
